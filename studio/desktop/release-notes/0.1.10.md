## What's new in 0.1.10

- **A much more fluid preview.** The preview now decodes, stabilizes, slows down and blurs each clip at the size it
  shows it, not at the full 4K of the file: a stabilized 4K Avata 2 frame costs the graphics card about a third of
  what it did, and a smooth edit's fast-slow-fast ramp with optical-flow slow motion and motion blur plays at about
  37 frames a second instead of 10 (M4 Mac), before any pre-render. **1/2** and **1/4** playback resolution now make
  those steps lighter too. Exports are drawn at full size, exactly as before.
- **Better words in every language.** About 280 sentences a language were written again in German, Spanish, French
  and Portuguese, and the effect names in Japanese, Korean and Chinese. Sixteen messages that showed no number, or a
  raw `{{value}}`, now show it.
- **Render preview, fixed.** Clearing the rendered stretch now frees its memory (up to 1 GB), and the button no
  longer stays busy after an error.
- **Looks.** `.cube` files that write their range as `0.0 1.0` import again.
- **Exports.** The save dialog's title is in your language, and an existing `.srt` subtitle file is never
  overwritten.
- **Steadier under the hood.** A stabilization cache write that fails half way leaves nothing behind, and the
  shadow pieces you saved no longer keep their memory after you close the project.
- **Safer app.** Electron's fuses are on (no `NODE_OPTIONS`, no debugger port in the release build), the editor's
  page runs under a Content-Security-Policy, links open in your browser only for the sites the editor links to,
  media paths refuse hidden and system folders, and the update check runs once every 24 hours even for an app you
  leave open.
- **The user guide button works in the app.** It did nothing in the desktop app; it now opens the guide in the
  window (**Open Studio** brings you back).
- **AI agents on a long profile path.** When the app's folder path is too long for a socket, the agent connection
  uses a short private one in `/tmp`, so "Allow AI agents" works there too.
