## What's new in 0.1.9

- **Ten languages, Italian included.** Studio now speaks Italian too, and the eight other languages show every
  string in their own words (85 were still in English). The built-in user guide is translated into all ten, and it
  opens in the language you chose.
- **Smooth edit in one click.** Put your clips and a song on the timeline and click **Smooth edit…** in the timeline
  toolbar: Studio finds the beats, cuts the clips to one, two or four bars each, and gives each the fast-slow-fast
  speed ramp with optical-flow slow motion, motion blur and an eased zoom, with a short crossfade at each cut. One
  undo step takes it all back.
- **Render preview, as in After Effects.** Mark a stretch with In and Out (or nothing, for the whole timeline) and
  choose **Render preview** in the timeline toolbar, or press Enter: Studio renders it ahead, a green line on the ruler
  shows what is ready, and that stretch plays at full frame rate. On a stabilized 4K Avata 2 clip with optical-flow
  slow motion and motion blur the preview went from about 9 to 60 frames a second (M4 Mac). Any edit that changes a
  rendered frame takes it off the green line and it is drawn live again; exports never use the rendered frames.
- **Playback resolution.** A Full, 1/2 or 1/4 button beside the proxy switch draws fewer pixels for each frame the
  preview composes. It does not speed up stabilization and optical flow, which work on the full source frame: for
  those, render the preview.
- **New versions, announced.** The app is not signed yet, so it cannot update itself; instead, when it starts (at
  most once a day) it asks GitHub for the latest version number and shows a small card when a newer one exists.
  It is the one request the app makes without you asking: **Settings › Privacy › Check for new versions** switches
  it off, and then no request is made.
- **TikTok and YouTube Shorts presets**, next to the Instagram Reel: vertical 1080x1920 H.264 at your project's
  frame rate, with the audio and bitrate each platform publishes.
- **DJI Mini 4 Pro and Air 3** are named in the clip panel, which says plainly that the D-Log M colour was fitted on
  Avata 2 footage and has not been checked on those drones.
- **Following the shadow** no longer fails with "no keyframe at or before source frame 0" on a file whose first
  frame is not at zero; a file with no keyframe at all gets a plain message, and nothing is charged.
- **AI agents.** `studio.clips.smoothEditTimeline` does the smooth edit in one call; `studio.preview` renders a range ahead and sets the playback resolution.
