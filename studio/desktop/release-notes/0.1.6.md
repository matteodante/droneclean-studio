## What's new in 0.1.6

- **Motion blur like ReelSmart Motion Blur.** Every pixel is now blurred along its own motion, measured by optical flow
  between the stabilized frames, over the shutter you choose: Clip › Video › Motion blur › **Amount**, from 0 to 1 in
  RSMB's units (0.5 is a 180° shutter, the default). It works at every speed: at normal speed it adds the smear a real
  shutter would, and in a sped-up clip the frames it covers join into one smear instead of the double image the old
  frame blend left (6 to 11 dB closer to a reference made of 64 interpolated frames, at 4x on a 4K Avata 2 clip).
  Projects saved with blur on keep their setting and are drawn the new way.
- **Smoother slow motion, Twixtor-style.** Clip › Video › Motion blur › Smooth slow motion › **Optical flow** now
  moves each pixel forward along its motion, decides which of two overlapping things is in front, and fills what was
  hidden: rebuilt frames are 1.0 to 3.1 dB closer to the real ones than 0.1.5's optical flow, and 5.6 to 9.6 dB closer
  than blending two frames, at the same speed. Thin peaks against the sky no longer tear. With Amount at 0 it is
  slow motion with no blur at all. Two controls appear under Optical flow: **Motion detail** (Normal, or High: the
  motion followed at twice the resolution, a little sharper on fast 4K footage and about three times slower to
  compute) and **Hard edges** (Mix frames, a soft halo where the motion cannot be read, or Nearest frame, no halo
  and a small local jump).
