## What's new in 0.1.7

- **Fade without the dark border.** The Fade transition shrank the outgoing clip, so a dark frame showed around
  full-screen clips while it played. The outgoing clip now zooms in a little instead, and the picture fills the frame
  all the way through; Fade keeps its gentle zoom and still looks different from Cross Dissolve.
- **Titles in proportion to the project.** A new title starts at 60 px on a 1080p project and in proportion on
  others (120 px on a 4K one), where before it came out at half that size in 4K.
- **AI agents.** The instructions point agents to `search` instead of the whole SDK reference, which is too long for
  one answer, and a contact sheet given columns and rows picks its own step to fit them.
