## What's new in 0.1.3

- **Instagram-ready MP4.** Every MP4 export now has its index at the front and no edit list, and the Instagram Reel
  preset writes AAC at 128 kbps, as Meta's Reel specification asks. Long exports still stream straight to the file.
  The preset itself now follows Meta's frame and bitrate: 1080×1920, 20 Mbps above 30 fps and 12 Mbps at or below.
- **Drone footage guide** (Docs › Drone footage): why a 30 fps clip should not be slowed down in a 60 fps project.
- **Optical-flow slow motion.** Smooth slow motion has a new mode, Optical flow, beside Frame blending: the frames
  in between are made from the motion, so a slowed clip shows single sharp detail instead of double images.
- **Smooth ramp + zoom.** One button in the speed panel gives a clip the drone-reel recipe: fast, slow, fast, with
  smooth slow motion, motion blur and an eased zoom-in.
- **Framing for vertical edits.** Two Framing sliders move the stabilized picture sideways or up and down, so a 4:3
  shot in a 9:16 project can keep its subject in view.
- **Speed curves with easing.** Each segment of a speed curve can be Linear, Smooth, Ease in or Ease out, and every
  frame still lands exactly where the curve puts it. The speed panel shows how many frames a second the clip really
  has at its slowest point and warns when that is fewer than the project plays.
- **Speed edits move less.** A clip that speeds down into a gap no longer pushes every clip after it; only what it
  now overlaps moves.
- **Mark beats.** Right-click a clip with sound and choose Mark Beats: a marker on every strong bass hit, to cut on
  the music.
- **Sky effect.** A graduated sky fill that lands on bright, washed-out skies and leaves the subject alone.
- **D-Log M asked once.** Importing DJI clips asks whether they were shot in D-Log M (the file does not say), and
  sets the colour profile on yes.
- **Stabilization fixed** after a clip is replaced with the same file: the editor no longer stalls, and an export
  that waits too long for a frame stops with a clear message.
- **Looks in the preview.** A look chosen on a paused clip always shows in the preview (it could stay undrawn).
- **Microphone.** Recording a voice-over asks for macOS's permission and says what to do if it is off, instead of
  waiting forever.
- **Fewer keychain prompts.** The app reads the shadow-removal sign-in only when you use shadow removal.
- **Wide projects.** Size and anchor fields show four- and five-digit values whole, and the keyframe graph's range
  follows the project's size.
- **For AI agents (MCP):** a stuck editor is stopped or reloaded by the server itself; `frame` keeps every render;
  `run` lists changes made after an `await`; an app started for an agent quits after ten idle minutes;
  `export.start({ name })` names the file; `clips.add` and `clips.addMany` refuse an overlap unless told to move
  (`onOverlap`); `clips.setLook` works without listing looks first; new `looks.rename`, `looks.remove`,
  `project.delete` and `markers.addBeats`.
